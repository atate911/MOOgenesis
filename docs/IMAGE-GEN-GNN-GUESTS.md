# Image-generation brief — GNN guest commentators

Some GNN bulletins have a **guest commentator**: a woman of one of the ten races who joins the neutral GNN host at the desk and
comments on the stories. She is the bulletin's glamour: shown alone in the **bumper** (the 10-second title opening) and then seated
at the desk beside the host. One outfit per guest, the same in both images.

## The look

Glamorous TV-guest styling: what a stylish pundit wears on a late-night cable show, somewhere between a night out and a broadcast
set. Eye-catching, confident and a little daring, pleasing to the eye, never explicit. Current fashion is the reference: fitted
cocktail and bodycon dresses, bodysuits, sheer layers, open backs, bare midriffs, braless tops, slits, cut-outs, heels, statement
jewellery. **Sheer is placed the way fashion places it**: embellishment, pattern, layering or folds cover what a broadcast would.
Every guest is an **adult woman**, poised and self-assured, styled for the camera. Never lingerie, swimwear or nudity.

She belongs to her species: the outfit is cut for her body (tails, wings, plumage, plating, carapace) and reflects her culture.

## Technical requirements

- **Bumper (one per guest):** landscape 16:9, **3840 × 2160** if possible (at least 2560 × 1440), a full scene: the guest in the
  GNN studio before air (the same studio as the anchor's openers: arched window onto an orbital station and a planet, brass
  pillars, the oval glass desk on a round dais, a large dark video wall). She is posed for the camera: on a stool by the desk,
  leaning on the desk edge, or walking onto the set. She fills about half the frame height, placed left or right of centre, and
  the **lower-left quarter stays calm** (the GNN titles sit there). Cinematic, photoreal, warm brass and cool blue light.
- **Desk (one per guest):** portrait 2:3, **1536 × 2304** (at least 1024 × 1536), on a **transparent background** (or flat
  #0B0F24), seated at a news desk seen from the front, from the waist up, the desk edge just below her waist, forearms resting on
  the desk, turned about 20° toward the **viewer's left** (the host sits to her right) and looking at the camera. Key light soft
  ivory from the upper left, cool cyan rim on the right. Same outfit, hair and jewellery as the bumper.
- No text, logos or other people in either image. Photoreal, 85 mm, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, no blown highlights.

## Delivery

Per guest: `gnn-guest-<race>-bumper.png` and `gnn-guest-<race>-desk.png` (e.g. `gnn-guest-mrrshan-bumper.png`). The other six races
(Sakkra, Psilon, Silicoid, Klackon, Bulrathi, Darlok) follow the same pattern; their prompts come next.
