# Image generation hand-off — alien race pictures for "Master of Orion — Reborn"

Paste this whole document to the image-generating AI. Goal: one consistent set of **ten race portraits** (plus a few extra shots per race, listed at the end) for a serious, grounded science-fiction strategy game.

## Art direction (applies to every image)
- **Tone:** serious, cinematic, grounded hard sci-fi — like concept art or a still from a modern AAA space-strategy game or a prestige sci-fi film. **Not** cartoon, not anime, not cute, not storybook, not kids' game, not chibi. No outlines, no flat colours, no big glossy eyes, no mascot proportions.
- **Realism:** believable anatomy and proportions; physically plausible materials (skin pores, fur strands, scales, chitin plates, brushed metal, crystal); realistic lighting — a strong key light from one side, soft fill, a coloured rim light, deep shadows; subtle depth of field; slight film grain; filmic, slightly desaturated palette with one accent colour per race.
- **Composition (portraits):** head-and-shoulders bust, three-quarter view, character looking just past the camera with a dignified, dangerous or thoughtful expression; the figure wears an imperial-style uniform or ceremonial dress suited to the race (high collar, insignia, no real-world flags or logos). Dark, out-of-focus background (a bridge, council hall, lab or ship interior) with a few soft light bokeh points. Leave the lower 15 % calm (UI text will overlay it).
- **Format:** square 1:1, **1024×1024 minimum** (2048×2048 preferred), PNG, no text, no watermark, no border, no UI. Plain dark vignette at the edges.
- **Originality:** completely original designs. Do **not** imitate any existing game's, film's or franchise's characters, logos or artwork (no Star Trek/Star Wars/Mass Effect/StarCraft/original Master of Orion art). Generic species archetypes only.
- **Consistency:** same lighting setup, camera distance, lens (≈85 mm), grading and level of detail for all ten, so they look like one series. Each race gets its own accent colour.
- **Avoid (negative prompt):** cartoon, anime, cel shading, outline, comic, illustration, 3D toy render, plastic skin, waxy face, uncanny valley, extra fingers, text, watermark, logo, signature, cute, chibi, oversized eyes, bright saturated rainbow colours, fantasy armour, medieval.

## The ten races (one prompt line each; accent colour in brackets)
1. **Human** [steel blue] — A weathered human diplomat-admiral, 50s, short grey-streaked dark hair, calm shrewd eyes, light stubble or clean-shaven, deep-blue naval-style uniform with silver braid. Trader's confident half-smile. Warm skin tones, believable pores and fine wrinkles.
2. **Mrrshan** [amber/orange] — Proud feline hunter-warrior, large cat-like head (lion/tiger anatomy, real fur with tabby stripes, whiskers, amber slit-pupil eyes, tufted ears), broad muzzle, scarred ear; dark gunmetal gunnery officer jacket with brass fittings. Predatory calm.
3. **Silicoid** [molten orange glow] — A living-crystal and rock being: faceted obsidian-and-quartz head and shoulders, cracks glowing with cooling lava, no mouth or a narrow fissure, two dim ember-like eyes set in a heavy brow; mineral plating, wisps of heat shimmer. Slow, ancient, heavy.
4. **Sakkra** [jade green] — Egg-laying saurian: lean reptilian head, fine overlapping scales (olive-green with darker dorsal ridge), a crest of small spines, yellow eyes with vertical slit pupils, forked tongue just visible; wears a harness-style military tunic. Aggressive, proud.
5. **Psilon** [pale gold/cream] — Hairless scholar with an enormous smooth cranium (about twice human height of skull), thin grey-yellow skin with visible fine veins, small mouth and nose, large dark calm eyes, a small jewel set in the forehead; plain high-collared scientist's robe. Serene, analytical, slightly unsettling.
6. **Alkari** [sky blue/white] — Proud avian raptor-people: hawk/eagle head with hooked beak, layered brown-and-cream feathers, sharp golden eyes, small feather crest; elegant flight-officer uniform cut around folded wing joints at the shoulders. Fierce, clan-proud.
7. **Klackon** [rust red/olive] — Ant-like insectoid: glossy chitin head plates in dark brown with rust highlights, large compound eyes, articulated mandibles, segmented antennae, fine bristles; plain utilitarian worker-caste collar. Orderly, expressionless, alien.
8. **Bulrathi** [forest green/brown] — Hulking bear-like warrior: broad dense-furred head (brown-black, grizzled), small intelligent dark eyes, heavy brow, wide muzzle with tusk-like canines, thick neck; huge shoulders in a plain trench-soldier greatcoat with medals. Slow-to-anger menace, hints of gentleness.
9. **Meklar** [cold cyan] — Cyborg: a gaunt humanoid face half organic, half machine — one eye a glowing lens, cheek and skull plated in brushed titanium with visible panel seams, cables into the collar, fused to a rigid exoskeleton shoulder rig; clinical expression. Industrial, precise.
10. **Darlok** [blood red/black] — Cloaked spy: a deep hood throwing the face into near darkness; only the faint outline of a sleek, shape-shifting face, with two small glowing red eyes; dark layered cloak with a faint wet-silk sheen. Mysterious, threatening, minimal detail on purpose.
