# Image-generation brief (v2) — race art for "Master of Orion — Reborn"

Paste this whole document to the image-generating AI. **Round 1 (ten bust portraits) is finished and approved** — those portraits are the visual anchor. Round 2 (below) creates the supporting scenes. For every Round-2 image, **attach that race's approved portrait** and ask the generator to keep the *same individual/species, colouring, uniform and insignia*.

---

## 1. What this is and why the pictures exist

**The game.** *Master of Orion — Reborn* is a browser remake of the classic 1993 space-strategy game *Master of Orion*. The player leads one of ten alien (and human) races: expanding across a galaxy of stars, managing colony economies, researching technology, designing warships, fighting tactical battles, negotiating with rival leaders, spying, and finally standing for election as Emperor in the Galactic Council. Rival empires are run by AI leaders with distinct personalities, so **the faces and rooms of these characters are the game's main source of drama** — you read their mood in the portrait before they speak.

**The feeling we want.** Serious, grounded, cinematic hard-science-fiction: the gravity of a prestige sci-fi drama or a modern AAA space-strategy game. A galaxy of proud, old, dangerous civilisations that have each been shaped by their homeworld, history and biology. Quiet menace, dignity, intelligence; wear and use on everything (nothing sterile or toy-like). It must *not* look cartoonish, cute, anime, storybook or like a kids' game. The interface around the pictures is a modernised echo of the 1993 original: bevelled bronze/leather-brown and gunmetal panels, gold type, deep indigo/black space. Pictures sit inside those frames, so **dark, low-key images with controlled highlights fit best** and avoid pure white.

**Where the pictures appear (the purpose of each shot is in §4).** Setup wizard (race selection), Races/Report screens (rival leader cards), the diplomacy **audience** screen (the leader talks to you, full screen), the Galactic **Council** chamber, the research/laboratory scenes, the news broadcast (GNN), encyclopedia, and the ending screens.

**Hard rules.** Completely original designs — never imitate any existing game, film or franchise's characters, logos, uniforms or artwork (including the original Master of Orion's art). Generic species archetypes only. No text, captions, logos, watermarks or UI in any image. No real-world flags or symbols. Keep the **same visual language** of the approved portraits: ~85 mm lens look, strong key light from one side, coloured rim light, deep shadow, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, muted filmic grade with ONE accent colour per race, believable materials (pores, fur, scales, chitin, brushed metal, crystal), a faint imperial-uniform language (high collars, small angular insignia, dull brass/silver trim).

**Creative freedom.** Everything below is lore and guidance, not a rigid spec. Invent architecture, props, textiles, weathering, small details of culture and history that *feel* inevitable for that species — as long as the species, colours and tone remain recognisable and the lore isn't contradicted. Surprise us, but stay inside the world.

**Technical.** PNG (or lossless WebP), no borders. Sizes per shot in §4. Keep the lower ~15 % of 1:1 and 2:3 shots calm (UI text overlays there) and leave a quiet area on one side of 16:9 shots (see each shot). Avoid blown highlights; keep mid-dark values.

**Negative prompt (use for everything):** cartoon, anime, cel shading, outlines, comic, illustration, toy, plastic, waxy skin, uncanny valley, mascot, cute, chibi, oversized eyes, text, watermark, logo, signature, rainbow saturation, fantasy armour, medieval, deformed hands, extra fingers.

Master prompt prefix: *"Cinematic concept-art still, photorealistic, grounded hard sci-fi, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, key light from the left, coloured rim light, deep shadows, subtle film grain, muted filmic colour grade, dark restrained palette,"*

## 2. The galaxy in one paragraph (shared lore)
Ten spacefaring peoples share a spiral arm. Long ago a vanished elder civilisation, the **Ancients**, left their throne-world **Orion**, guarded by a monstrous automated Guardian; whoever rules the Galactic Council is called "Master of Orion". Races have standing grudges (Mrrshan↔Alkari blood feud; nearly everyone distrusts the Darlok), old trade ties, and very different ideas of honour. Technology looks *industrial and lived-in*: riveted plating, pressure doors, holo-tables, amber and cyan instrument glow, cable runs, brass-and-steel fittings. Each race's architecture and fashion is an extension of its biology and homeworld.

## 3. The ten peoples (traits are the game's real strengths/weaknesses — let them shape the look)

For each: **role in the game**, **strengths / weaknesses** (what the design should *hint*), **body & dress**, **surroundings**, **personality & mood range**, **accent colour**, **creative hooks**.

### 1 · Human — homeworld Sol — accent steel-blue
- **Game role:** the diplomats and traders; the "friendly" default. **Strengths:** expert traders (trade treaties pay more), magnificent diplomats (everyone starts friendly, Council votes count extra), cheap force-field science. **Weakness:** no martial, production, growth or spy edge — they win by deals and shields.
- **Body & dress:** ordinary human variety; the approved leader is a weathered 50-something admiral-diplomat. Deep navy naval-style uniform, silver braid, discreet insignia; civilian formal wear for audiences (dark tailored coats, a small comm-pin). Never overtly militaristic.
- **Surroundings:** an orbital capital: warm-lit wood-and-steel offices, trade-hall mezzanines, big windows onto Earth-blue planetlight, commodity tickers, ship models, treaty tables with brass fittings. Pragmatic, comfortable, a little worn.
- **Personality:** charming, shrewd, amused, quietly tough. Moods: warm-genial → polite-wary → cold-courteous.
- **Hooks:** a diplomat's pouch of gifts, mismatched souvenirs from many alien courts, a chess-like board game left mid-play.

### 2 · Mrrshan — Fierias — amber / orange
- **Game role:** gunnery masters, bad diplomats. **Strengths:** deadliest shooters (ships hit far more often, act first), cheap weapon research. **Weaknesses:** poor engineers (construction costs more), prickly — five races start uneasy, the Alkari hate them.
- **Body & dress:** big feline predators (lion/tiger anatomy: mane-ruff, tabby stripes, whiskers, amber slit eyes), digitigrade, powerful shoulders, expressive ears and tail. Dark gunmetal gunnery-officer jackets with brass fittings; ceremonial sashes, claw-guards, kill-tally tassels. Proud, theatrical.
- **Surroundings:** open-plan hunting-lodge architecture on a ship or palace: tiered gunnery decks, trophy walls of targeting scopes and old weapon barrels, sun-warmed stone and brass, long sightlines (they like seeing prey/targets from a distance), dust motes in amber light.
- **Personality:** arrogant, honour-bound, restless; respects marksmanship. Moods: lazy-predatory calm → insulted fury.
- **Hooks:** gun-cleaning rituals, a fine rifle as a diplomatic "gift", scorched trophies from the Alkari war.

### 3 · Silicoid — Cryslon — molten orange (on obsidian/quartz)
- **Game role:** the "settle anywhere" race. **Strengths:** immune to every environment (colonise any world), no pollution, no plague. **Weaknesses:** very slow breeders, narrow science (no environment techs, most fields costlier). They speak rarely and strike early.
- **Body & dress:** living crystal and cooling basalt; faceted obsidian plates, quartz spines, glowing magma fissures that brighten with emotion (dim = calm, white-hot = anger). No mouth, or a narrow glowing fissure. Wears mineral-plated regalia: heavy collars of forged metal ringed with heat-shimmer, almost no cloth.
- **Surroundings:** warm, dim, volcanic architecture: black glass galleries, slow-flowing lava channels cut into floors, steam vents, heat haze, crystal-lit halls with extreme stillness. Equipment scorched.
- **Personality:** glacial patience, ancient, rare words. Moods: dormant-ember calm → sudden incandescent anger.
- **Hooks:** a crack that slowly glows during conversation, a "calendar" of mineral growth rings, silence as a threat.

### 4 · Sakkra — Sssla — jade green
- **Game role:** the expansionist breeders. **Strengths:** explosive population growth, cheap planetology (terraforming). **Weaknesses:** strained relations with several races; poor diplomacy; need constant new land.
- **Body & dress:** saurian: lean, long-necked, crested, fine overlapping olive/green scales with a darker dorsal ridge, yellow slit eyes, forked tongue; tail. Military harness over a dark high-collar tunic, bronze buckles, clan bands on the arm. Practical, aggressive.
- **Surroundings:** steaming jungle-world architecture grown into the ship: humid corridors with condensation, moss-stained bulkheads, hatchery galleries with rows of warm incubator pods glowing amber-green, vines through rusty ironwork.
- **Personality:** proud, impatient, prizes strength; hospitable only to the strong. Moods: restless menace → sneering disdain → hungry attention.
- **Hooks:** egg-clutch tallies as status, a crowded nursery in the background, a hunting spear as a ceremonial staff.

### 5 · Psilon — Mentar — pale gold / cream
- **Game role:** the scholars. **Strengths:** superior researchers (×1.5 output, cheaper fields, a wider tech tree). **Weakness:** no military or production edge — the galaxy would rather out-shoot them.
- **Body & dress:** hairless, enormous smooth cranium with fine vein tracery, small mouth/nose, large dark calm eyes, a small jewel set in the forehead (a mark of scholarly rank). Simple high-collared scholar's robes in cream and bronze, thin gold edging, small data-jewel pendants. Elegant, understated.
- **Surroundings:** vast quiet libraries and laboratories: white-gold stone and bronze lattice, floating holographic star-charts and equations, long shelves of data crystals, still pools reflecting light; clean but intensely used.
- **Personality:** serene, curious, faintly condescending, unsettlingly analytical. Moods: absorbed calm → polite impatience → pained disappointment.
- **Hooks:** unsolved-problem walls (equations on glass), a half-built orrery, tea-like ritual drinks.

### 6 · Alkari — Altair — sky blue / white
- **Game role:** ace pilots. **Strengths:** ships are much harder to hit and act earlier; cheap propulsion research. **Weaknesses:** weak force-field science; blood feud with the Mrrshan and wary of several others.
- **Body & dress:** raptor-people: hawk/eagle head with hooked beak, layered brown-cream plumage, sharp golden eyes, folded wings or wing-joints at the shoulders (can glide). Elegant flight-officer uniforms tailored around the wings; blue-and-white colours, clan crests, talon-guard bracers, flight goggles pushed up.
- **Surroundings:** high-altitude aeries: cliff-top spires, open-air landing platforms, wind-stirred banners, vaulted windows over cloud seas, perches and rafters inside halls, hangars full of sleek fighters.
- **Personality:** proud, clan-minded, quick, honour-sensitive. Moods: alert/precise → proud contempt → blazing feud-rage (Mrrshan).
- **Hooks:** clan banners of fallen pilots, feathers kept as honours, a wind-tunnel training hall.

### 7 · Klackon — Kholdan — rust red / olive
- **Game role:** the hive workforce. **Strengths:** every colonist produces double, cheap construction, nearly no rebellion. **Weaknesses:** short-range drives, insular and mistrusted.
- **Body & dress:** large ant-like insectoids: glossy dark-brown/rust chitin, big compound eyes, mandibles, segmented antennae, fine bristles, multiple limbs (keep a humanoid-ish upright bust). Plain uniform worker-caste collars, caste glyph plates, tool-belts; almost no personal ornament — individuality is rare and suspicious.
- **Surroundings:** an orderly hive-city: honeycomb corridors lit amber, ranks of identical stations, pheromone-vent glows, conveyor "trails" along walls, vast factory floors; clean, repetitive, efficient; clay/resin textures mixed with industrial steel.
- **Personality:** orderly, impersonal, courteous in a mechanical way. Moods: flat neutrality → antennae-twitching alarm.
- **Hooks:** a queen-caste chamber glimpsed beyond, rank shown by antenna rings, identical figures behind the speaker.

### 8 · Bulrathi — Ursa — forest green / brown
- **Game role:** unmatched ground fighters. **Strengths:** troops fight with a huge bonus, cheap construction and weapons research. **Weakness:** poor computers. Slow to anger, slower to befriend — gardeners at heart who tame hostile worlds.
- **Body & dress:** hulking bear-people: dense brown-black grizzled fur, small intelligent eyes, heavy brow, wide muzzle with tusks, enormous shoulders. Plain trench-soldier greatcoats in green-olive wool and leather, brass buckles, rows of campaign medals; practical gardening tools sometimes tucked in a belt.
- **Surroundings:** a fortress-farm: stone and timber with riveted steel, greenhouse domes of hardy plants, bunkers beside terraced gardens, wood-fired iron stoves, maps of campaigns pinned beside seed catalogues. Cold, snowy light through thick windows.
- **Personality:** stoic, dry, loyal, blunt. Moods: grumbling patience → ominous quiet → terrifying controlled anger.
- **Hooks:** a medal-case beside a bonsai, a trench-coat with soil on the hem, a hand-carved chess set.

### 9 · Meklar — Meklon — cold cyan
- **Game role:** automated industry. **Strengths:** factories run extra per colonist; cheap computing. **Weakness:** poor planetologists. Efficient assembly lines; "least predictable councils".
- **Body & dress:** cyborgs — a humanoid face half organic, half titanium; one glowing lens eye, panelled skull, cables into the collar, exoskeleton shoulder rigs, extra mechanical arms or tool-limbs. Severe black coats over their machinery, thin cyan piping, serial-number plates instead of insignia.
- **Surroundings:** colossal automated foundries: assembly arms in constant motion, molten-metal glow and blue-white arc light, gantries, conveyor rivers, status screens everywhere, oil-sheen floors, ordered noise. Council halls are bare steel rooms with rows of terminals.
- **Personality:** precise, clinical, occasionally erratic when "logic" disagrees with a vote. Moods: efficient neutral → cold impatience → glitch-like flicker of strain.
- **Hooks:** a spare limb on a workbench, maintenance staff of drones, a visible repair seam being welded.

### 10 · Darlok — Nazin — blood red / black
- **Game role:** master spies. **Strengths:** spies cost half and are far better at stealing and catching; sharp computers. **Weakness:** every race (except Humans) starts uneasy toward them. They trade in secrets.
- **Body & dress:** a hooded, shape-shifting figure — the face is mostly shadow, only a suggestion of a sleek, sinuous visage and two small red eyes; layered dark cloaks with a wet-silk sheen, concealed blades, small silver clasps shaped like keys or eyes. Hands rarely seen; gloves.
- **Surroundings:** shadowy: narrow candle-and-neon back-rooms, one-way mirrors, curtained alcoves, dossier walls with red string and pinned photos, ventilation fog, rain on dark glass, a single red-lit lamp; data-slates tucked everywhere.
- **Personality:** polite, patient, never quite honest. Moods: velvet courtesy → faintly amused menace → unnervingly still.
- **Hooks:** several silhouettes blurred behind them (the leader may be someone else), a door that is slightly ajar, a hand holding a coin that isn't there.

## 4. Round-2 shots — purpose and context (per race: 5 shots, 50 images)

Always: attach the race's approved portrait; same character, uniform, insignia, accent colour; same lighting language.

### A. Audience scene — 16:9, 1920×1080 — *the most important one*
- **Purpose/context:** shown full-screen when the player talks diplomacy with this race's leader (proposing treaties, trade, threats; or the AI contacts the player). The leader is drawn **separately** in the foreground (the existing bust portrait), so this image is the **hall/room behind them** — **no character in it** (or a distant out-of-focus silhouette at most). A bottom-right panel of menu text and a speaking leader will overlay the **left 45 % and bottom 30 %**, so keep the left side and bottom calm and slightly darker; put the visual interest (architecture, light shafts, windows, art) on the centre-right.
- **Content:** the race's seat of power, culture and homeworld mood per §3 *Surroundings* — an audience hall/throne-office/war room appropriate to them, with depth, a window or backdrop hinting at the homeworld, and a dominant accent-colour light source.
- **Mood:** imposing and a little intimidating; reads as "you are a guest here".

### B. Scientist at a console — 16:9, 1920×1080
- **Purpose/context:** appears in the Research screen and the "breakthrough" moments: when the player's scientists discover a technology or choose the next project, a scene of **a scientist of the player's race** working at a console is shown. The right 40 % is overlaid with tech names and descriptions, so keep the scientist and console on the **left–centre**, with the right side darker/uncluttered (holo-glow, equipment).
- **Content:** one scientist (not the leader — a different individual of the same race, same dress language but lab/technical version: coat, apron, goggles, tool-harness appropriate to the species) at a lab console with holographic readouts, specimens, star-charts or schematics. Convey the race's science style: Psilon equations and floating orreries; Meklar machine diagnostics; Mrrshan ballistics rigs; Klackon rows of identical workers; Silicoid crystal-resonance rigs; etc.
- **Mood:** focused discovery; a hint of triumph or unease; lit by screen glow with a warm key.

### C. Full-figure leader — 2:3 portrait, 1024×1536 (2048×3072 preferred)
- **Purpose/context:** used in the **setup wizard** (big race picture beside the race's stats and traits), the **Council chamber** (standing/seated delegate), the **encyclopedia** and **ending** screens. A tall, clean image of the entire leader, standing, three-quarter view, dignified pose revealing their body structure, dress and culture. Dark neutral background with only a faint hint of the setting (so it can be cut out and placed on other backdrops); feet visible, soft floor reflection, nothing cropped.
- **Content:** show the *anatomy honestly* (tail, wings, extra limbs, posture), full formal dress, and 2–3 telling props (see *Hooks*).
- **Mood:** confident, iconic — "the face of the race".

### D. Homeworld from orbit — 16:9, 1920×1080 (and a 1:1 crop-safe version)
- **Purpose/context:** setup wizard race page and the colony/system screens use a homeworld picture to tell the player "this is where they come from". Planet **in the lower-left or lower-right third**, a star glinting on the limb, thin atmosphere glow, city lights or signature features on the night side, a few ships/stations in orbit in the race's architectural style. Leave dark empty space top and right.
- **Planets:** Sol (blue oceans, human city-light webs, orbital shipyards); Fierias (savannah-gold continents, vast hunting reserves, sunlit amber); Cryslon (black crust, glowing magma seams, crystalline ring-sparkle); Sssla (cloud-wrapped steaming green jungle world, thunderstorms); Mentar (pale gold-white planet with elegant lattice-like orbital observatories); Altair (high mountain world, thin air, floating-cloud seas, towers like aeries); Kholdan (rust-coloured world covered in ordered hive-lattice patterns visible from orbit); Ursa (cold forest-and-tundra world, snow caps, dark conifer belts); Meklon (grey-industrial world, entire continents of glowing foundries, orbital rings of factories); Nazin (dusky world, perpetual dark rain, a few scattered red lights, hidden orbital stations).

### E. Council seat / diplomat portrait — 1:1, 1024×1024
- **Purpose/context:** the Galactic Council is a ring of alcoves where each race's delegate sits and votes (votes count by population). This portrait is a **seated delegate in formal council dress** (more ceremonial than the leader bust: sash, chain of office, banner colour of their empire) seen slightly from above/the side, in a dim domed chamber with their alcove lit by a pool of accent-coloured light. It is displayed small (≈200 px) beside vote tallies, so the silhouette and accent colour must read clearly at thumbnail size.
- **Content:** the *same* leader as the portrait, seated, hands visible (resting, gesturing, or holding a ballot token), alcove architecture matching §3 *Surroundings*.

### Optional extras (only after A–E are approved)
- **Spy/advisor portraits:** one lesser-ranked character per race (advisor or spy handler), same style, 1:1.
- **Expressions:** the leader bust in **pleased**, **angry** and **neutral-wary** variants (same pose and lighting, only the expression and light temperature shift), for diplomatic reactions.
- **Warship:** one flagship per race in orbit (16:9), hard-surface industrial style reflecting the race's architecture (Mrrshan: gun-heavy, brass; Alkari: sleek winged fighters; Klackon: modular hive-barges; Silicoid: rock-crystal hulls; Meklar: spiky automated foundry-ships; Darlok: dark, angular, low-signature; Sakkra: organic-looking, bone-and-bronze; Psilon: elegant gold-white lattice; Bulrathi: blunt armoured slabs; Human: practical grey-blue cruisers).
