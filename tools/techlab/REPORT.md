## 2. Twin trials

Paired copies of the same game; one empire gets the tech in both, with its effects switched off in the control copy (so both get the same tech-level bump). Effect = treatment − control of that empire, as log ratios (≈ percent). EPI = 0.4 production + 0.25 population + 0.2 fleet + 0.15 tech level. Verdicts place each tech among the classic techs within six levels of it: top quarter = strong, middle half = typical, bottom quarter = low; outside the whole classic range = an outlier. Situational = the tech changed nothing in half the trials or more (mostly combat and defence techs: AI wars are rare in the 50 turns after the grant; their worth in battle is the calibration above).

## 7. Caveats

* Every number here is from AI-only games. Situational techs (minefields, lifeboats, warp damping, salvage, landing craft) can matter more in a player's hands than their AI numbers show.
* In the leagues most games end at the council around turn 125-150, so techs above level ~35 are rarely researched there; their twin trials (granted at turn 120, measured to turn 170) are the evidence for them.
* The empire power index counts hulls, not their quality: classic ship-component techs read slightly negative (better components make ships pricier), so for combat effects the calibration in section 1 is the fairer measure.
* Chaotic games make late checkpoints noisy: the first league seed set read ships at turn 200 as −20 %, the replication as +19 %; pooled, +4 % with a CI that spans zero.
