# Phone layout layer — October 2026 (branch `claude/phone-mode`)

The game plays on a phone. Desktop is untouched: every phone rule is gated on `<html data-phone>`, and
`tools/qa/layout-fingerprint.js` shows identical desktop layouts to `main` (3 window sizes × 17 screens).

Built in three rounds. Round 1 (P0–P5) made every screen work on a phone. Round 2 (R1–R6) fixed what the interface review found:
targets and text too small, a bottom bar that hid half its buttons, a colony sheet that showed only 3 of its 5 sliders at half
height, tool screens three phone-screens long, and information that hid behind an undiscoverable long press or talked about keys.
Round 3 gave the phone a native-app look with the game's skin (the user picked it from three rendered mockups: light touch, native
layout + game skin, fully platform-like), plus haptics and installed-app polish.

## How it is wired

| Piece | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Gate | `index.html` inline script | `window.MOO_PHONE` + `<html data-phone>` before any CSS loads: coarse pointer AND `min(screen.width, screen.height) ≤ 600` (phones in either orientation; tablets keep the desktop layout). `?phone=1` / `?phone=0` force it. |
| Load-time value | `js/core/phone.js` | `MOO.Phone.boot / active() / forced`. Headless-safe (off in Node). Perf, workers and audio read it **once at start-up**. |
| UI plumbing | `js/ui/phone.js` | The app bar (Back + the screen's title), history sentinel, tooltips on touch (long press, tap-to-read, disabled buttons, touch wording of tip texts), `UI.Phone.say / key` for visible hints, `UI.Phone.sheet()` and `UI.Phone.stepper()` widgets, F1 overlay off, autosave on `pagehide`. |
| Galaxy | `js/ui/phone-galaxy.js` | The tab bar (galaxy.js's own buttons, tagged + a More tab), the side panel as a bottom sheet driven by `UI.Side.show` / `refresh` wrappers, the summary line, the content-measured half height, the first-run card; map overview key → sheet. |
| Research | `js/ui/phone-research.js` + `UI.Tech._phone` in `tech.js` | Allocation · Project · Techs segments (`data-ph-seg` on `.tch`). |
| Ship design | `js/ui/phone-design.js` + 3 hooks in `design.js` | Re-arranges the editor's own containers; card picker; weapon steppers; library sheet. |
| Rules | `css/phone.css` | The phone LAYOUT. Every rule under `html[data-phone]`; `html:root:root[data-phone]` where a bumped (`:root:root`) restyle must lose. |
| Skin | `css/phone-skin.css` | The phone LOOK (round 3), same gate, loaded after phone.css: family tokens, flat cards, controls, app bar, tab bar, dialogs, toasts. |

**Sizes.** Root font `clamp(15px, min(4.1vw, 2.6vh), 18px)` (15px floor, so `.8rem` is never under 12px; landscape sits on the
floor). Tokens on `html[data-phone]`: `--tap: max(44px, 2.75rem)` for buttons and controls, `--row: max(38px, 2.4rem)` for list rows,
`--ph-strip` for the collapsed sheet (the minimap, map key and toasts sit above it). Labels the stylesheets set under `.75rem`–`.85rem`
carry `max(12px, …)` floors, grouped by their own size (an `:is()` list takes its most specific member's weight for every member, so a
floor can never be lower than the size it replaces). Slider tracks stay slim (24px: a tall `touch-action:none` track swallows vertical
scrolls); arrows and locks are `--tap` squares. The Scale setting stops at 110% on phones.

**Skin (round 3).** Native structure, the game's skin. Each screen family keeps its colours through `--pk-*` tokens set on its root
(`--pk-surface / card / edge / sep / head / ink / ink2 / btn / btn-edge / press / well / sel / bar`): indigo by default (menus, dialogs,
setup, options, almanac), gunmetal (`.gx`, `.ov`), grey metal (`.dz`), bronze (`.lp`, `.lf`), brown leather (`.tch`, `.rx`, `.ttp`).
Generic rules then read the tokens:
- screen bezels and sheet frames are gone; panels, ledger boxes and race cards are flat cards (face + 1px edge + soft shadow);
- section headers are small Oxanium caps on the card (no plate); row labels are sentence case Plex Sans with hairline rows;
- buttons are flat (face + 1px edge, darker when pressed); the primary is a gold plate; the base rules outrank every `:hover` rule, so
  nothing stays lit after a tap;
- sliders keep the red arrows with a visible knob; switches are larger without ON / OFF; tabs and segmented controls are wells with
  a gold selected plate;
- the app bar has a plain-text "‹ Back" and a centred title; the tab bar is flat with a gold Next turn; the galaxy HUD is a top bar
  over a grouped map-control column; the galaxy sheet has rounded top corners (the only rounded corners) and a light grip;
- dialogs have a plain title and full-width footer buttons; toasts are snackbars;
- the title menu keeps its gold words on the art; setup and Options lose the title and Back the app bar already shows; the design
  preview's "drag to turn" hint is gone (it collided with the part labels).
Specificity: rules are `html:root:root[data-phone]` (0,4,1) against bumped restyles (0,4,0); `html:root:root:root[data-phone]` only
against theme-v2's primary / active / selection rules (0,6,0).

**Haptics.** `UI.Phone.haptic(kind)`: `tap` 8 ms, `step` 5 ms (at most every 45 ms, so a slider drag ticks), `snap` 10 ms, `turn` 20 ms,
`warn` 20-40-20. Android uses `navigator.vibrate`; iOS has no vibration API, so on iOS 18+ a hidden `<input type=checkbox switch>` is
toggled (the system plays its tick; only inside a tap, elsewhere nothing happens). Settings `haptics` (on by default), Options ›
Haptics on phones. Sites: Next turn (`galaxy.js`), slider steps (`kit.js`), switches and segmented controls (`setup.js`), sheet snaps
by the player, long-press tips (UI and map), error toasts (`kit.js`).

**Installed app.** `theme-color` follows the screen on phones (galaxy HUD, design header, app bar, title art; `phone.js`). The
manifest has an `id` and the game's background colour. iOS launch images (`assets/icons/launch-WxH.png`, ten iPhone sizes, rendered by
`tools/make-icons.js`, linked in `index.html`) stop the installed app opening on a white screen. Notch and home-indicator padding on
the HUD, tab bar and app bar; no text selection or iOS callout on a long press (fields excepted).

**Back.** One control dispatches a synthetic `Escape` keydown: every screen, modal, popup and panel already handles Escape exactly
like its right-click, so nothing per-screen was needed. The Android/iOS back gesture pops a single history entry (re-armed on the
next real tap, never inside `popstate`) and does the same. On the title screen the entry is not armed, so back leaves the page.
The control lives in an app bar on every screen except the map, the menu and the design editor (`UI.Phone.backHidden`), with the
screen's title on the right (a fixed map in `phone.js`; Save / Load follows the screen's own heading). `<html data-ph-bar>` is set
before a screen's `enter()` (Screens.go is wrapped) so canvases measure their final size. Because the bar carries the title and Back,
`phone.css` hides the in-screen big titles and the OK / Done / Back buttons that only close the screen (Transfer, Scrap, Equalise,
Full scale, Prev / Next, Audience and setup's steps stay).

**Galaxy.** Fixed tabs: Planets · Fleet · Tech · Races · More, then a wide Next turn; icon over label (beside it in landscape).
They are galaxy.js's own buttons laid out by CSS (`.turn-locked` keeps working); Game, Design and Map are hidden and live in More,
which is the game menu (`gamemenu.js`) as a bottom sheet with Ship design and Galaxy map added at the top. The side panel is a
sheet: collapsed, it shows a title row with a thumbnail (the colony's planet; a star's planet as last seen with its *known* owner —
`Q.planetCard` carries the real one; or the star itself) and the summary line (`.ph-sum`: a colony's pop and growth, production,
what it builds and when, one warning; a star's planet, owner and range). A tap anywhere on the strip opens it halfway (captured, so
the rename prompt opens only from half / full). Half height is the controls — the five sliders and Ships / Reloc / Trans as a row —
measured from the content (at most 70% of the map); the picture, stats, build box and rally line wait for full. A floating panel pins
to the bottom edge; a pick-a-star panel opens halfway (Fleet deployment keeps Cancel / Accept sticky); a held prompt opens fully and
the earlier height comes back after. Landscape keeps the desktop's right-hand column (no summary, no thumbnail). The map control
column is two wide in landscape.

**Tooltips on touch.** A long press (450 ms) on any `[data-tip]` shows it. A plain tap shows it too when the tap does nothing else:
from the target up to the tip holder nothing is a control (`button, a, input, label, [role=…], [tabindex], canvas`), carries
`data-act` (set by `UI.h` for click / pointer handlers — `kit.js`) or has a pointer / grab / move / resize cursor; a second tap closes
it; Options › Tooltips off leaves the long press only. A disabled button (no pointer events) is found by its box under the finger.
Tip texts lose hotkeys (`<b>F2</b>`, `(R)`, `· key 1`) and clauses about the wheel, right-click, hover or modifier keys, and Click
becomes Tap (`UI.Phone.touchText`). On the galaxy map a finger picks stars within 22px (the nearer of star / fleet wins), drags start
after 8px, and a still 450 ms press shows the star / fleet tooltip above the finger; it stays after the lift until the next touch and
that release selects nothing (`galaxymap.js`, phone + touch only).

**Wording.** Visible hints that name keys or the mouse go through `UI.Phone.say(desktop, phone)` (a no-op on desktop) or, for key
badges, `UI.Phone.key(k)` (Space → Tap, Esc → Back, letter / digit keys → dropped). Sites: council (`setHint`, demo line), landing
(`KEY`), endings (hint), research choice scene (hint), research allocation foot, galaxy map route hint and fleet-group tip, fleet
ledger scrap banner, planets transfer banner, Reloc / Trans / Fleet deployment pick hints, races prompts, star system return hint,
design library foot, Options tooltip row, the first tutor card, the Almanac Controls tab (a touch table). Key badges (`.cou-card .key`,
`.tch-tab .kb`, `.rc-key`, `.au-item kbd`, `.btn .kbd`) and hotkey underlines (`u.hk`) are hidden by CSS. A first-run card
(Settings `phoneHints`) explains the strip, long press / tap, Back and More.

**Research.** Allocation (the six sliders, the field name on its own line so a tap can lock it, Equalise) · Project (the field tabs —
a 2-column grid, all six in view — and the project card) · Techs (tabs, list, detail; Overview's tree lives here). Leaving Techs from
Overview returns to the field view; entering with `view: 'overview'` or tapping Overview / an "available next" link selects Techs.

**Combat.** Phones never open the tactical screen: the pre-battle card offers Retreat and Auto only (`combat.js` buildPreBattle),
`Combat.fight` maps a fight to auto, and the combat screen's `enter` auto-resolves and returns if a phone lands on it.

**Perf / audio / workers.** `TIERS.phone` (dpr 2, GL on, few particles) sits outside `ORDER`: the governor never climbs into it and
leaves it only for `low` after 3 slow seconds; a phone on a software renderer starts `low`. Worker pool capped at 2. The sampled
orchestra is off (its 840 files decode to hundreds of MB; iOS kills tabs near 384 MB) — the synth voices play. Options on a phone has
a Performance overlay row (`perf.js` now follows the `perfOverlay` setting live) and no News mode row (phones always get the brief list).

**Installable.** `manifest.webmanifest` + `assets/icons/*` (rendered by `tools/make-icons.js`) + Apple tags in `index.html`.
No service worker. The save server's gate lets the manifest and icons through (browsers fetch them without cookies);
`scripts/lib.sh` deploys both; the nginx template serves the manifest with its type.

**Desktop one-liners** (all no-ops unless `MOO.Phone.active()`): `combat.js` (card, fight, screen enter), `options.js` (battle and News
rows, overlay row, Scale cap, tooltip wording), `flow.js` (brief news), `design.js` (renderPopup hand-over, row hook, `UI.Design._phone`,
library foot), `system.js` (`geometry()` portrait, return hint), `tech.js` (`layoutScene` portrait, `UI.Tech._phone`, two hints),
`spyscenes.js` (tap to read, tap again to steal), `races.js` (tap highlights on Racial stats, prompts), `gamemenu.js` (More entries +
sheet class), `side-colony.js` / `side-misc.js` (title thumbnails, pick hints), `galaxymap.js` (finger picking, long-press tip, hints),
`council.js` / `landing.js` / `endings.js` / `ledger-*.js` / `help.js` (wording), `galaxy.js` (Next turn haptic), `setup.js` (switch /
segmented haptic), `workers.js`, `sampler.js`, `perf.js`. Shared files: `js/manifest.js` (7 entries), `js/ui/kit.js` (`UI.tipHtml` export,
`data-act`, slider and error-toast haptics), `js/gfx/icons.js` (`more`), `js/core/settings.js` (`phoneHints`, `haptics`), `index.html`
(launch images), `manifest.webmanifest`.

## Testing

```
node tools/qa/phone.js                 # portrait phone (390x844): gate, galaxy sheet + tab bar + More, battle cards, 17 screens,
                                       # research segments, scenes, menus, design editor, Back + hardware back, tooltips (long press,
                                       # tap-to-read, disabled buttons, map long-press), first-run card, 4 bot turns; then a desktop pass
node tools/qa/phone.js landscape       # the same on an 844x390 phone
node tools/qa/phone.js small           # the same on a 360x740 phone
node tools/qa/modes.js phone phoneland phonesmall   # the robustness matrix (8 bot turns) on phone profiles
node tools/qa/layout-fingerprint.js > a.json; node tools/qa/layout-fingerprint.js <main checkout> > b.json; diff a.json b.json
```
On every screen and dialog the phone QA also fails on touch targets under 40px (36px for rows 100px+ wide, 24px for slider tracks),
visible text under 12px, and visible keyboard / mouse wording (click, Esc, Space-as-a-key, wheel, hover, F-keys, Ctrl), and checks the
app-bar title and that no OK / Done / Back of the screen's own is showing. The `native` step stubs `navigator.vibrate` and checks the
haptics (slider step, Next turn, none when switched off; none on desktop), `theme-color` per screen, that a tapped button keeps its
look (no stuck hover) and setup's single Back and title. Phone contexts preset Settings `phoneHints` (the `firstrun`
step tests the card). Screenshots land in `$SHOTS` (default `/tmp/claude-0/phone`). The emulated phone renders in software, so it runs
the `low` tier; on a real phone GPU it is `phone`. Known console noise the QA filters: offline web fonts, software-GL driver chatter,
`assets/art/still/inst-spaceport.webp` (listed in ART-NEEDED.md), and `api/status` on a static server.

## Accepted losses on phones

Keyboard-only actions with no touch equivalent: F4/F5 (colonies with ships), Shift+I (attention backwards), Home, Ctrl± research nudge,
GNN previous story (phones get the brief list), F1 context help (the Almanac's Controls tab has a touch table instead). Covered by
existing buttons: colony cycling (title arrows), attention forward (HUD chip), relocate-all (Reloc › All colonies), Esc-clears (Back),
hull 1–4 / B / C, double-clicks (the map's double-tap still opens a star's system view). Landscape keeps the single-column design
editor and tool screens (cramped in 390px of height: see follow-ups).
