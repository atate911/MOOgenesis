# Brief: Tactical combat screen (+ pre-battle card, auto results)

**Read `briefs/_ui-common.md` first.** Spec: `research/06-ui-presentation-audio.md` §6.1 (pre-battle panel), §6.2 (tactical screen: 10×8 grid, bottom command bar Auto / Planet / Scan / Missile / Special / Retreat / Wait / Done, turn model, FX list), `research/03-ships-combat.md` §4 (rules the UI must expose faithfully: initiative order, movement = Chebyshev, "all weapons that reach fire", hit chances, missiles in flight, retreat), `js/sim/combat.js` (another agent is writing it — its **interactive tactical API** is the contract: `Combat.create/legalMoves/targets/act/endRound/aiTurn/isOver/finish`; read its header comment as soon as the file appears and re-test against it; until then build against fixtures shaped as documented in `briefs/combat.md`), `Gfx.Ships`, `Gfx.Fx` / `Gfx.GLFx`, `Gfx.Scenes.battle`, `Gfx.Planets`, `Audio.SFX` (laser, missile_launch, missile_hit, explosion_*, shield_hit, armor_hit, deathray, warp …), `Audio.Music.play('combat1'|'combat2')`.

## You own
`js/ui/combat.js` (`MOO.UI.Combat`, screen `combat`), `js/ui/combat-fx.js` (optional helper).

## API & behaviour
- `MOO.UI.Combat.fight(battle, S) → Promise<result>` (called from `Game.handlers.battle`): 1) the **pre-battle card** in the galaxy side panel ("Space Combat — <attacker> attacks <defender>", planet shown, fleet strengths, an odds estimate from a Monte-Carlo of `Combat.resolve` across the worker pool (`MOO.Workers.map('resolveBattle', …)`), buttons **Continue (fight)**, **Auto**, **Retreat** where legal); 2) the **tactical screen**: 10×8 grid over `Gfx.Scenes.battle` (star + planet + nebula/rocks per battle data), your ships on the left, enemy on the right, the planet with its missile bases on the defender's side. Units are `Gfx.Ships.draw` sprites with stack counts and HP bars; the active unit has a selection frame; legal move cells highlighted; hovering an enemy shows the **real hit chance** (`Combat.targets`); click to move/attack as in the original; **Wait / Done / Retreat / Scan / Missile toggle / Special / Planet info / Auto** buttons + keys (A P S M X R W D/Esc); beams, missiles (8 directions, speed), bombs, shields flashes, explosions scaled to damage, floating damage numbers, warp-out for retreating ships — all driven by the `events[]` returned by `Combat.act/endRound`, animated at `Settings.animSpeed` (0 = instant), skippable. Scan = the full list of enemy ship stats (defense, missile defense, level, HP, shield absorb, weapons, specials). Planet info overlay (pop, factories, bases, shield, HP). The enemy side is driven by `Combat.aiTurn`. When the battle ends show a summary card (losses per side, survivors, retreats, bombardment/invasion prompts if the module reports them) and resolve the promise with `Combat.finish(B)`.
- **Auto**: resolve immediately with `Combat.resolve` (same result shape) and show only the summary.
- Cinematic flair within reason: camera shake on big hits, glow/bloom via Fx, music `combat1/combat2` with `Music.setIntensity` rising as ships die. Performance: ≥ 60 fps with 200 units on 'high' tier; use the GPU Fx when `MOO.Gfx.GL.supported()`.
Test via fixtures (a helper in your file `MOO.UI.Combat.demo()` creating two fleets with the Design API) and `?dev=1&screen=combat`.
