# Brief: Races, Report, Status and the Audience (diplomacy conversation)

**Read `briefs/_ui-common.md` first.** Spec: `research/06-ui-presentation-audio.md` §5.14 (Races screen), §5.15 (Report), §5.16 (Status), §5.17 (Audience), §5.18 (spy scenes — the spy UI on the Races screen belongs to you; the steal/sabotage scenes belong to another agent), `research/05-diplomacy-events-victory.md` §4.4/§4.8 (menu structure, message catalogue), `js/data/text.js` (`Data.text.diplomacy[personality][situation]` — our own dialogue lines), `js/sim/diplo.js` (another agent is writing it: `Diplo.status/menu/propose/declareWar…`; read its header when it appears; until then fixture data), `js/sim/q.js` (`Q.status`), `js/data/races.js`, `Gfx.Portraits`, `Gfx.Scenes.audience`.

## You own
`js/ui/races.js` (screens `races`, `report`, `status`), `js/ui/audience.js` (screen `audience` + `MOO.UI.Audience`).

## Screens / API
1. **`races`** — up to 5 rival slots (2×3 grid like the original): portrait (`Portraits.canvas`), race name plate in banner colour, treaty text (No treaty / Non-aggression pact / Alliance / War) and trade text ("No trade" / "n BC per year"), the **relations bar** (red→green gradient with pointer and the 17-step label: Harmony … Feud), **spy-network slider** (readout "No spies / n spies"; order `spyBudget {target, value}`; cost per spy shown) with **Hide / Sabotage / Espionage** mode buttons (`spyMode`), "No Contact" / "Not present" / destroyed states, bottom: **Overall security** slider (`security` order, "+n%") and "Allocations: n% of planetary resources" total; buttons **Status / Report / Audience / OK** (choose an empire by clicking its portrait or keys 1–5).
2. **`report`** (params `{empire}`) — that empire's portrait, name plate, **personality + objective** ("Xenophobic Ecologist"), report age ("Report 15 / 72 years old"), Alliances and Wars lists, and six boxes (Computers … Weapons) of the techs you know they have (those you also have in normal colour, those you lack highlighted).
3. **`status`** — "Racial Stats" with the year and six bar groups (Fleet strength, Population, Technology, Planets, Production, Total power), one bar per empire in banner colour, yours first (data: `Q.status`); modern touch: animated bars + hover values + small history sparkline.
4. **`audience`** (params `{empire}`) and `MOO.UI.Audience.show(interrupt) → Promise` (for AI-initiated audiences from `Diplo.turn`) / `MOO.UI.Audience.open(aiId)` (player-initiated from the Races screen): the diplomatic scene — your hooded emperor seen from behind in banner colour, the alien ambassador as a hologram/portrait with three mood states (calm / pleased / angry; `Portraits.draw` mood), dialogue panel with typed text in the AI's personality voice from `Data.text.diplomacy`, and the original's menus: **Propose Treaty** (Non-Aggression Pact / Alliance / Peace Treaty / Declare War on Another Race / Break Alliance With Another Race), **Form Trade Agreement** (amount list), **Threaten / Break Treaty and Trade**, **Offer Tribute** (BC amounts or a technology), **Exchange Technology** (their techs → your offers), **Good Bye**; show counter-offers ("sweeteners") with Agree / Forget It. Call `Diplo.menu/propose/…` for every action (never decide outcomes yourself); show outcomes, relation changes and any 'ambassador recalled' state. Music `Audio.Music.play('race:<id>')` (calm vs hostile variant by attitude).
Test with `?dev=1&screen=races&opp=4&turns=30` (contact needs time/proximity; for fixtures call `MOO.Sim.Diplo` helpers or set `S.empires[0].relations[1].contact=true` from the console).
