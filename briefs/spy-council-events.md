## Specification (implement exactly what §5–§8 describe)
### `MOO.Sim.Spy`
Budget & hiring (`E.spyBudget[target]` per-mille of gross, `E.spies[target]`, cost `25 + 2×Computer TL`, doubling per extra spy, Darlok halved, `buildTurn`), modes per target (Hide/Espionage/Sabotage in `E.spyMode[target]`), per-turn resolution `turn(S,R)` (success/caught rolls with computer TL, Darlok +30/+20, security, frame-ups, spies lost), the espionage mission (steal tech: candidate pool, cap by `rmax`, human gets a choice → return interrupts `{type:'spyStealChoice', candidates:[…]}` answered with a pick; AI picks per its rules), sabotage (destroy factories / missile bases / incite revolt, framing a third party), reports (`Spy.reports(S,E)` for the Spies-Caught screen) and consequences via `Diplo.incident`. Orders: `spyBudget {target,value}`, `spyMode {target,mode}` (security already exists in Econ). AI behaviour hook: `Spy.aiPlan(S, E, R)` → orders (research/01 §5.9, research/05 §5.7).
### `MOO.Sim.Council`
`due(S)` (≥ 2/3 of stars colonised, then every 25 years while no emperor, > 2 empires alive), `convene(S,R) → info` (electorate, vote weights = pop/100 rounded up, min 1, the two largest as candidates, human votes last), AI voting per research/01 §5.10 & research/05 §6, `autoVote(S,info)` for headless, `resolve(S, info, humanVote, R) → {winner, votes, accepted…, gameOver, reason}` incl. relation effects, the human accept/defy decision (return an interrupt-able structure: `{needsAnswer:'accept'|'defy'}` handled by a second call `Council.finish(S, info, accepted)`), the Final War (all others ally, share techs) and elimination of defeated/refusing parties.
### `MOO.Sim.Events` and `MOO.Sim.Artifacts`
The yearly event engine (chance `max(0, year − lastEventYear)/512` scaled by difficulty, victim weighting, once-per-game flags), ALL sixteen events with their parameters/announcements (plague, quake, nova, industrial accident, comet, pirates, computer virus, derelict, rebellion, fertile/rich/poor planet, donation, assassination/diplomatic blunder, space crystal/amoeba monsters moving through the map …) pushing `{type:'news', …}` events plus presentation interrupts the UI can show (return them from `turn(S,R)` as `{type:'event', kind, …}`), the planet-level event timers (`planet.event`) and cures/payments the human can make (order `eventCure {planet}`), `coups(S,R)`, monsters (`S.monsters` list with positions/HP by difficulty, movement, effects on planets, interaction with `Combat.findBattles`), the Orion Guardian (stats by difficulty are consumed by Combat; you handle the loot when `S.guardian.alive` becomes false: Death Ray + up to 3 techs per research/04 §3.9 & research/02 §1.10), and `Artifacts.loot` (first ship-explorer gets a tech from an artifacts planet, research/02 §1.10).
